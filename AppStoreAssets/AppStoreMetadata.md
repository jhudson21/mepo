## 4. Full App Description
```text
Mepo is your personal memory journal and location-aware task companion, designed to keep what matters most anchored to the places you love.

Whether saving a tucked-away coffee shop in Paris, capturing a family memory in the park, or setting a reminder to buy groceries the moment you arrive, Mepo bridges your memories and your daily to-dos into one liquid glass experience.

KEY FEATURES:

• MEMORIES & PLACES
Pin memories with photos, custom ratings, notes, and tags. Filter by categories like Food & Drink, Travel, Cafes, and Notes, or browse your life on an interactive map.

• DAY-TO-DAY SMART REMINDERS
Stay on top of your tasks with priority tags, recurring schedules, and smart geofences that notify you automatically upon arrival or departure.

• DIRECT MAP NAVIGATION
Tap any mini-map preview to instantly get turn-by-turn directions in Apple Maps.

• HOME SCREEN & LOCK SCREEN WIDGETS
Glance at your top memories and check off pending tasks directly from your Home Screen with interactive WidgetKit widgets.

• FAST VOICE & QUICK ACTIONS
Dictate thoughts on the go with real-time speech recognition or use Home Screen 3D Touch quick actions to capture ideas instantly.

• SPOTLIGHT INTEGRATION & EXPORT
Search your memories directly from iOS Spotlight search. Export your full archive anytime in JSON or Markdown.

• PRIVACY-FIRST BY DESIGN
Your thoughts belong to you. Mepo stores all your memories and reminders locally on your device with SwiftData and iCloud sync. No accounts required, no trackers, and zero third-party ads.
```
